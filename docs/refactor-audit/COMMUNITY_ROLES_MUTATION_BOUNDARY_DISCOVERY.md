# Community Roles Mutation Boundary Discovery

Active role mutation occurs in self-role setup/select, guest cleanup, welcome onboarding, MemberGuard release/lockdown, Concierge quick links, and Night Crew unlocks. The runtime performs role creation, hierarchy checks, member add/remove, settings JSON writes, and permission visibility synchronization.

Roles cannot be assigned solely to Community: MemberGuard owns safety decisions, onboarding owns first-arrival behavior, and Panels own some interaction entry points. A future slice must first distinguish role reads/recommendations from role configuration, creation, assignment, and repair.
