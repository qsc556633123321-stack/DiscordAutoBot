# Community Publication Channel Identity Contract

Setup selects the first exact-name text-channel cache match. Persisted channel IDs
are written after publishing but are not setup lookup inputs. Type participates in
lookup; topic, position, permission overwrite, and parent are not identity keys.
Guide moves a name-matched channel to its expected parent; Roadmap only ensures its
category/channel. Duplicate names, stale IDs, deleted/recreated channels, and manual
changes have no semantic resolver or repair path.
